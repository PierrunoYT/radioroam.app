About Radio Roam

Free online radio from every corner of the world

Radio Roam is a free internet radio player that brings live stations from more than 200 countries into one place. Tune in to pop, rock, jazz, electronic and classical music, catch up on news and talk radio, or follow live sports commentary, straight from your browser.

Is Radio Roam free?

Yes. Radio Roam is completely free, with no account, subscription or download required.

Where do the radio stations come from?

Stations come from Radio-Browser, a community-maintained directory of more than 30,000 internet radio stations worldwide.

Can I listen to local radio stations?

Yes. Choose Near me to see stations from your country, or search by city, region or country name.

Does Radio Roam work on my phone?

Yes. Radio Roam runs in any modern browser on phones, tablets and computers, and can be added to your home screen.
